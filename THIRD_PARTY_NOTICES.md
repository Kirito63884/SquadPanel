## SquadJS

SquadPanel uses SquadJS (https://github.com/Team-Silver-Sphere/SquadJS) as its reference
implementation. The following parts are ported to TypeScript from SquadJS source:

- `packages/rcon` — RCON framing quirks (multi-packet "empty packet" handling, the broken
  trailer packet after an empty command), the `Online IDs` parser, and the response/event
  regexes for `ListPlayers`, `ListSquads`, `ShowCurrentMap`, `ShowNextMap`, chat, warn,
  kick, ban, squad-created and admin-camera events (`core/rcon.js`, `core/id-parser.js`,
  `squad-server/rcon.js`).
- `packages/logs` — `SquadGame.log` regexes (`squad-server/log-parser/*.js`).
- `apps/worker` — the plugin concept (event-driven plugins such as teamkill auto-kick,
  auto messages, team balance and Discord integration) is modelled after SquadJS plugins.
